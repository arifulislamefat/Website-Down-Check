Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information isitdownnow.site ("the site," "we") collects and how it's used. This is a small, independently run tool — if anything here is unclear, use the contact details at the bottom.

The URLs you check

When you enter a URL to check, our server makes a live request to that address to determine if it's reachable. We do not store, log, or share the URLs submitted through the checker.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic to the site. It's cookieless and doesn't track individuals across other websites — it only provides aggregate numbers like page views and approximate location by country.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you'll see a consent prompt on your first visit letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads.

Cookies

Aside from cookies that Google's advertising services may set (covered above), this site does not use its own tracking or advertising cookies.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated terms.
